- Facial and Jaw Relaxation
	The Vagus nerve is closely "bundled" with the Cranial Nerves that control the muscles of the face and jaw (Cranial Nerves VII and V).
	
	- **The Cue:** Intentionally dropping the jaw (creating space between teeth) and softening the muscles around the eyes.
	- **The Science:** There is a **bi-directional loop** between facial muscles and the brainstem. If the jaw is clenched, neuroception detects "predation" or "defense." When the jaw is slack, it signals the "Rest and Digest" state because a mammal cannot eat or rest while in a fight.

- **Vibroacoustic Therapy (VAT)**
- 


Once the Vagus nerve is stimulated, it inhibits the **HPA axis** (the system that pumps out stress hormones). Within **5 to 20 minutes**, you will see a measurable drop in **Salivary Alpha-Amylase**.
However, **Cortisol** is "sticky." It takes about 20–60 minutes for the liver to clear it from your bloodstream.


While individual results vary based on your "vagal tone," scientific studies show the following:

- **Deep Pressure/Weighted Blankets:** Research indicates a **33% reduction** in electrodermal activity (stress arousal) and a significant decrease in self-reported anxiety within 20 minutes [1].
- **Affective Touch (CT-Afferents):** Studies on gentle stroking show a **significant blunting** of the cortisol response to a stressor compared to those with no touch. It acts as a "buffer" that keeps cortisol from spiking in the first place [2].
- **Vagal Stimulation (VNS):** Clinical-grade stimulation can reduce inflammatory markers (Cytokines) by **30-40%**, which are the chemical "messengers" that tell the brain to stay in a state of threat [3].
