
# The inner experience of time [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2009.0003)
- Psychological research has shown that cognitive functions such as **attention**, **working memory** as well as **long-term memory** determine our temporal judgements ([Brown 1997](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib15); [Zakay & Block 2004](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib164); [Taatgen _et al_. 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib137)). Moreover, drive states, moods and emotions ([Wittmann _et al_. 2006](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib161); [Droit-Volet & Meck 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib37); [Noulhiane _et al_. 2007_a_](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib104)) as well as factors of **personality** ([Rammsayer 1997](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2685813/#bib119)) influence **duration estimates**.
