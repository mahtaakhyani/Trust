>[!note]  Notes: 
>M2 selects and sequences the motor programs needed for complex actions. While M1 executes the movement, M2 decides how to perform it. 
>M2 is located in the [frontal lobe](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/23073-cerebral-cortex), anterior to the [precentral gyrus](https://radiopaedia.org/articles/38614?embed_domain=hackmd.io%252525252f%2525252540yipuafecsl2jsu8smr5njq%252525252fbnjhjgjghjghjghfavicon.ico) (M1). 
>M2 receives significant input from the [secondary somatosensory cortex (S2)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10350168/). This allows the brain to adjust planned movements based on current sensory feedback (e.g., adjusting your grip on a slippery object). #cognitive/anxiety/state_anxiety/bio/somatosensory_cortex/postcentral_gyrus ^tr-ibrscaq4j
