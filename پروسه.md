 Psychological pain acts the same way as physiological pain [(Mee et al., 2006)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychires.2006.03.003). So minimizing both is important for wearable robot interactions. Negative emotions such as anxiety, fear, stress can be categorized into Psychological pain. thus, minimizing them is beneficial.
