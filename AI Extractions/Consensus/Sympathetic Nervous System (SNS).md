The sympathetic nervous system (SNS) is a key part of the autonomic nervous system responsible for the "fight or flight" response, preparing the body for physical activity by regulating functions such as **heart rate**, **blood flow**, and **metabolism**.  #cognitive/anxiety/bio/SNS #cognitive/anxiety/bio/SNS/fight_or_flight 
#measures/objective_measures/HRV 
It establishes precise connections between postganglionic neurons and peripheral organs to maintain homeostasis during activities like exercise and temperature regulation ([[#^f22730|Scott-Solomon et al., 2021]]; [[#^06230f|Alshak & Das, 2019]]). ^tr-wvmq7hwt9

Additionally, the SNS influences skeletal muscle function and aging-related sarcopenia by regulating motor innervation and neuromuscular junction stability ([[#^bf2db0|Delbono et al., 2021]]).  #cognitive/anxiety/bio/SNS/skeletal_muscle_function ^tr-rqie2juay

The SNS also modulates inflammatory processes in chronic diseases such as osteoarthritis via neurotransmitters like **norepinephrine**, which can have both pro- and anti-inflammatory effects depending on context ([[#^beb0d9|Sohn & Jenei-Lanzl, 2023]]). #chemicals/neurotransmitters/Norepinephrine
