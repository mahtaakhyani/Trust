Misuse and disuse in wearable robots are closely linked to the dynamics of over-trust and under-trust. Over-trust can lead to misuse, where users may rely excessively on the robot’s capabilities, potentially causing safety risks or inappropriate applications, while under-trust results in disuse, where users avoid or underutilize the technology due to lack of confidence or perceived unreliability (Menolotto et al., 2024; Sharkey & Sharkey, 2020).
